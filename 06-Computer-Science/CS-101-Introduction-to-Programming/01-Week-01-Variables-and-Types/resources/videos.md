## 🎯 What to Watch For

When watching videos:
1. **Pause and code along** - don't just watch
2. **Take notes** - write down key concepts
3. **Experiment** - try modifying the examples
4. **Rewatch** - complex parts may need multiple views

## ⏱️ Recommended Schedule

- **Day 1-2**: Watch overview videos (30-40 min)
- **Day 3-4**: Watch detailed tutorials (60 min)
- **Day 5-7**: Practice with exercises

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Watch at 1.25x or 1.5x speed if comfortable
- Use YouTube's playback speed controls
- Take breaks every 20-30 minutes
- Don't watch more than 2 hours without coding practice
